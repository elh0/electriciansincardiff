Questions

How quickly will an electrician call?

Usually the same or next working day. For no power or a burning smell, turn off at the fuse board and say so in the notes.

Is it really free?

Yes. We're paid by the electrician we work with, not by you. You only pay for work you agree to.

Will lots of firms call me?

No. Your request goes to one local electrician only.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes for notifiable work like a new fuse board or rewire. Ask for the certificate on the quote.
